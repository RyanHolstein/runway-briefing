Most of it's flying out of Newark, because of course it is. We're talking Luxembourg, Marseille, Ibiza, Valencia, places like that. Some of these are seasonal, some are year round. They're also adding Okinawa out of San Francisco and Toulouse out of Dulles.

So United's basically going where nobody else is going. Smart move if you can fill the planes.

It's smaller than what United did, no question. But it's American saying, hey, we're still playing this game too. Philly's clearly getting some love as a European gateway this year.

Basically what you're seeing here is both airlines making their big swing for next summer at the same time, which almost never happens by accident.
